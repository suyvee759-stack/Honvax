WHY IT MATTERS

A weak RFQ produces quotations that cannot be compared.

Technically similar products can represent very different scope, materials, standards and acceptance criteria.

The first objective is therefore not to obtain a price quickly, but to establish what suppliers are actually being asked to quote.

KEY REVIEW DIMENSIONS

What needs to be made clear.

01Confirmed information

Requirements clearly supported by the RFQ, BOQ, drawings or client confirmation.

02Assumptions

Items temporarily assumed for evaluation and clearly marked so they are not mistaken for confirmed requirements.

03Missing information

Critical inputs that prevent reliable supplier enquiry or comparison.

04Sourcing practicality

Order size, timing, approval route, logistics and service factors that may affect the import case.
