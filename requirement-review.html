WHY IT MATTERS

A weak RFQ produces quotations that cannot be compared

Supplier sourcing should not begin with an incomplete product description and an assumed specification.

The initial review separates confirmed requirements from assumptions and clarification points. This reduces wasted enquiries, misleading prices and late technical surprises.

WHAT THE REVIEW CAN PRODUCE

A practical basis for deciding whether to proceed

01

Requirement summary

A structured view of product scope, quantities, standards, delivery expectations and available technical evidence.

02

Clarification points

Questions and missing inputs that should be resolved before meaningful supplier quotations are requested.

03

Initial sourcing view

A preliminary recommendation to develop China sourcing, obtain more information or consider local supply.

WHAT TO SEND

Use the information already available

The first review can start with incomplete documents, provided the missing information is made visible.

01RFQ or BOQ

Product descriptions, quantities, scope boundaries and commercial instructions.

02Technical documents

Specifications, drawings, data sheets, standards and approved-brand requirements.

03Delivery requirement

Destination, required date, packaging, site constraints and preferred delivery basis.

04Commercial context

Project stage, quotation deadline, expected order timing and known budget constraints where available.

COMMERCIAL BOUNDARY

The initial review is deliberately limited

Initial assessment may include

  • China supply feasibility
  • Major missing information
  • Obvious technical or logistics risks
  • Recommendation on whether to continue

Formal work may include

  • Detailed technical clarification
  • Supplier development and factory engagement
  • Formal quotation and cost development
  • Supply, inspection and delivery execution
