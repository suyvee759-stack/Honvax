WHY IT MATTERS

A weak RFQ produces quotations that cannot be compared

Supplier sourcing should not begin with an incomplete product description and an assumed specification.

The initial review separates confirmed requirements from assumptions and clarification points. This reduces wasted enquiries, misleading prices and late technical surprises.

Requirement record

Separate facts from assumptions.

Confirmed

Supported by the enquiry

Record the stated quantities, standards, dimensions and document revisions, together with their source.

Assumption

Needs explicit agreement

Mark any proposed interpretation, material or delivery basis so it cannot silently become part of the order.

Missing

Needs clarification

Identify unanswered questions, who should resolve them and which prevent a meaningful quotation.

The resulting requirement summary and clarification list help suppliers quote the same scope. A vague enquiry can produce apparently competitive offers for different products.

WHAT TO SEND

Use the information already available

The first review can start with incomplete documents, provided the missing information is made visible.

01RFQ or BOQ

Product descriptions, quantities, scope boundaries and commercial instructions.

02Technical documents

Specifications, drawings, data sheets, standards and approved-brand requirements.

03Delivery requirement

Destination, required date, packaging, site constraints and preferred delivery basis.

04Commercial context

Project stage, quotation deadline, expected order timing and known budget constraints where available.

Detailed deliverables depend on the agreed supply or service scope.
