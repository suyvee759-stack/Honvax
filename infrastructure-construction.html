What the RFQ Should Define

Information needed before a reliable sourcing decision.

Material & dimensionsGrade, profile, thickness, tolerances, dimensions and applicable product standards.
FabricationApproved drawings, welding requirements, procedures, tolerances and trial assembly.
ProtectionGalvanizing, painting, lining, coating and corrosion-category requirements.
Quality evidenceMill certificates, inspection points, testing, traceability and document format.
Delivery constraintsPackaging, container loading, site access, required date and local-content requirements.
Sourcing Decision

The right supply route depends on more than factory price.

Stronger candidates

  • Planned packages with consolidated volume
  • Fabricated items supported by approved drawings
  • Products with efficient container loading characteristics
  • Requirements where China offers manufacturing or fabrication depth

Requires early caution

  • Urgent site purchases or partial-load quantities
  • Low-value commodities already competitive locally
  • Bulky items with poor shipping density
  • Products subject to mandatory local-content requirements
