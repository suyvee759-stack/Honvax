When China sourcing makes sense

Planned, consolidated and specification-led packages have the strongest potential. Low-value commodity items, partial truckloads and short-notice site purchases may lose their price advantage after freight and handling.

Bid decision principle

Proceed only when specification compliance, landed cost, lead time, payment exposure and after-sales responsibility are commercially acceptable together.
