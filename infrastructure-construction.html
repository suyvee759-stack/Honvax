WHERE HONVAX FITS

Import value often comes from the package, not one commodity item

Steel and construction materials can be price-sensitive, heavy and locally competitive. Freight, loading efficiency, fabrication, coatings and site delivery can quickly change the commercial result.

HONVAX assesses whether a coordinated China package offers a credible advantage over local supply. Products are developed around the BOQ, drawings and project schedule, with landed cost and execution risk considered before quotation.

WHAT THE RFQ SHOULD DEFINE

Information needed before a reliable sourcing decision

Technical scope and logistics must be evaluated together for heavy or fabricated materials.

01

Material and dimensions

Grade, profile, thickness, tolerances, dimensions and applicable product standards.

02

Fabrication

Approved drawings, welding requirements, procedures, tolerances and trial assembly.

03

Protection

Galvanizing, painting, lining, coating and corrosion-category requirements.

04

Quality evidence

Mill certificates, inspection points, testing, traceability and document format.

05

Delivery constraints

Packaging, container loading, site access, required date and local-content requirements.

SOURCING DECISION

Low factory price does not guarantee a competitive delivered package

Stronger candidates

  • Planned packages with consolidated volume
  • Fabricated items supported by approved drawings
  • Products with efficient container loading characteristics
  • Requirements where China offers manufacturing or fabrication depth

Requires early caution

  • Urgent site purchases or partial-load quantities
  • Low-value commodities already competitive locally
  • Bulky items with poor shipping density
  • Products subject to mandatory local-content requirements
