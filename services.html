Scope of work

Initial Review vs Formal Work

Initial Review

An initial view of China supply maturity, major technical or delivery risks, missing information and whether further sourcing work is worthwhile. A brief enquiry is enough to start.

Formal Work

Supplier engagement, detailed technical clarification, quotations, comparison, inspection and execution proceed under the agreed supply or service arrangement.

Supplier contacts, original supplier quotations and HONVAX purchasing prices are not included in the initial review. Any disclosure forms part of the agreed engagement.
