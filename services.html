COMMERCIAL BOUNDARY

Initial review vs formal sourcing work.

The first review is deliberately limited. Its purpose is to decide whether further China sourcing work is justified.

Initial review may include

  • China supply feasibility
  • Major missing information
  • Obvious technical or logistics risks
  • Recommendation to proceed, clarify or consider local supply

Formal work may include

  • Supplier development and factory engagement
  • Formal quotations and technical clarification
  • Supplier comparison, samples or inspection
  • Procurement, export and delivery execution
Commercial information boundary: supplier contacts, original supplier quotations, HONVAX purchasing prices and detailed sourcing work are not part of the initial assessment unless specifically agreed.
