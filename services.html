Services built around the actual RFQ

The work and commercial arrangement depend on the product, technical risk, order value and support required.

01

RFQ & BOQ review

Review of scope, quantity, drawings, specifications, standards, delivery requirements and missing information.

02

China supplier sourcing

Identification and screening of potential manufacturers against the specific requirement.

03

Technical & commercial comparison

Structured comparison of compliance, exclusions, lead time, payment terms, warranty and price.

04

Product supply

HONVAX may quote and supply an agreed package, including coordinated purchasing and export execution.

05

Quality coordination

Inspection planning, document collection, pre-shipment checks and third-party inspection coordination where required.

06

Export & logistics support

Packaging, consolidation, export documentation, freight and delivery coordination based on agreed scope.

Where free review ends

The initial review is deliberately limited and intended to decide whether further sourcing work is justified.

Initial assessment may include

  • China supply feasibility
  • Likely sourcing route
  • Major risks and missing inputs
  • Recommendation to proceed or source locally

Formal work may include

  • Supplier development and direct factory engagement
  • Detailed quotation and technical clarification
  • Factory or supplier information
  • Inspection, expediting, export and delivery execution
