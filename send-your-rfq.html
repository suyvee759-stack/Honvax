What Happens Next

  1. Review
    We assess the available information and whether a suitable China supply route is worth developing.
  2. Clarify
    We may request missing details, propose a quotation route or recommend a different purchasing approach.
  3. Confirm Next Step
    We confirm whether to proceed to a quotation or agree a defined procurement assignment. Formal work follows the agreed scope and commercial terms.

Sending an enquiry does not commit you to an order or paid sourcing work.

Commercial Confidentiality

Your drawings, pricing information and project documents are treated as commercially confidential and used to assess and respond to your enquiry.

Relevant technical details may be shared with prospective suppliers where needed for an agreed sourcing enquiry. Tell us about disclosure restrictions, or request a non-disclosure agreement before sending sensitive material.
