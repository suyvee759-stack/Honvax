What the RFQ Should Define

Information needed before a reliable sourcing decision.

Identity & fitDrawings, samples, dimensions, tolerances, machine model and known part references.
MaterialComposition, hardness, heat treatment, coating and required mechanical properties.
Operating dutyFeed material, impact, abrasion, temperature, load and service environment.
Quality evidenceInspection plan, material tests, dimensional reports, traceability and sample approval.
Commercial contextAnnual usage, failure history, required date, target order quantity and shutdown schedule.
Sourcing Decision

The right supply route depends on more than factory price.

Stronger candidates

  • Repeat parts supported by drawings, samples or reliable references
  • Planned shutdown and maintenance packages
  • Requirements where sample or trial-order approval is possible
  • Volumes that justify tooling, inspection and supplier development

Requires early caution

  • Urgent breakdown parts needed immediately
  • Unknown equivalents without drawings or samples
  • Safety-critical items with insufficient technical evidence
  • One-off low-value parts with high development cost
