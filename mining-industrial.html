WHERE HONVAX FITS

A part number alone may not explain why a component succeeds or fails.

For wear parts and industrial replacement components, fit, material composition, hardness, heat treatment, tolerances and operating duty may matter more than a general product name.

Repeat programmes can create strong value when the part can be verified through drawings, samples, material requirements and trial supply.

WHAT THE RFQ SHOULD DEFINE

Information needed before a reliable sourcing decision.

01

Identity and fit

Part number, drawing, sample, dimensions, tolerances and interface details.

02

Material

Grade, composition, hardness, heat treatment, coating and required certificates.

03

Operating duty

Wear mechanism, impact, load, temperature, speed and surrounding process conditions.

04

Quality evidence

Inspection method, material test reports, dimensional checks and traceability.

05

Commercial context

Annual consumption, trial quantity, shutdown timing and cost of failure.

SOURCING DECISION

The right supply route depends on more than factory price.

Stronger candidates

  • Repeat wear parts with reliable drawings, samples or references
  • Planned screening or maintenance programmes
  • Items that can be trialled before full-volume adoption
  • Packages with sufficient volume and lead time for controlled development

Requires early caution

  • Urgent breakdown parts required immediately
  • Safety-critical parts without a clear validation route
  • Items where failure cost is high and operating duty is poorly understood
  • Small one-off quantities with high development effort
