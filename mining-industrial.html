WHAT THE RFQ SHOULD DEFINE

Information needed before a reliable sourcing decision

The more accurately the duty and failure mode are understood, the more meaningful the supplier comparison becomes.

01

Identity and fit

Drawings, samples, dimensions, tolerances, machine model and known part references.

02

Material

Composition, hardness, heat treatment, coating and required mechanical properties.

03

Operating duty

Feed material, impact, abrasion, temperature, load and service environment.

04

Quality evidence

Inspection plan, material tests, dimensional reports, traceability and sample approval.

05

Commercial context

Annual usage, failure history, required date, target order quantity and shutdown schedule.

SOURCING DECISION

Development effort should match the value of the requirement

Stronger candidates

  • Repeat parts supported by drawings, samples or reliable references
  • Planned shutdown and maintenance packages
  • Requirements where sample or trial-order approval is possible
  • Volumes that justify tooling, inspection and supplier development

Requires early caution

  • Urgent breakdown parts needed immediately
  • Unknown equivalents without drawings or samples
  • Safety-critical items with insufficient technical evidence
  • One-off low-value parts with high development cost
