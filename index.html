A lower-risk first step

Not every requirement should be imported from China.

What we can assess initially

  • Whether the supply category has a mature China supply base
  • Whether the order size and delivery window support importing
  • Major specification, certification and logistics risks
  • Whether China sourcing is worth taking to formal quotation
Good procurement advice can include “buy locally”.

Urgent, low-value, small-quantity or service-intensive requirements may be better sourced in South Africa. We aim to identify that early.

Have an RFQ, BOQ or specification?

Send the available documents. We will review whether the requirement is suitable for China sourcing and identify the information needed for a reliable quotation.
