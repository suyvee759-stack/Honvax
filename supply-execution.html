WHY IT MATTERS

A purchase order is the start of execution, not the end of procurement control.

Approved drawings, quality requirements, packaging, documentation and shipping responsibilities must remain aligned through production and shipment.

The execution plan is matched to the risk of the product and the responsibilities agreed in the order.
