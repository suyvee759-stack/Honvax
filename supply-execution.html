WHY IT MATTERS

A confirmed quotation is only the beginning of delivery risk

Production changes, late approvals, missing documents, inadequate packaging and unclear logistics responsibilities can damage an otherwise sound sourcing decision.

HONVAX coordinates the agreed execution plan with the supplier and client. The exact scope is defined before order placement and may range from document follow-up to complete product supply and delivery coordination.

POTENTIAL EXECUTION SCOPE

Controls matched to the product and order risk

01

Order and production control

Confirmed order scope, approved documents, milestone tracking and coordination of supplier clarification.

02

Quality coordination

Inspection planning, document collection, pre-shipment verification and third-party inspection coordination where agreed.

03

Export and delivery

Packaging, consolidation, export documentation, freight and delivery follow-up according to the agreed delivery basis.

EXECUTION CONTROLS

Responsibilities should be clear before the purchase order

01Approved baseline

Final technical scope, drawings, quantities, commercial terms and documented deviations.

02Quality plan

Required records, hold points, inspection method, acceptance criteria and responsible parties.

03Shipment plan

Packaging, marking, consolidation, Incoterms, export documents and freight responsibility.

04Delivery and close-out

Destination, receiving requirements, final document set, warranty route and outstanding actions.

SCOPE BOUNDARY

  • Packaging and export-document coordination
  • Freight and delivery follow-up

Must be agreed before order

  • Incoterms, insurance and customs responsibilities
