THE BACKGROUND BEHIND HONVAX

Built on project procurement in China and international markets

Each core team member brings more than 20 years of procurement experience from engineering, EPC contracting and industrial supply-chain environments. The team's background includes extensive purchasing work for major projects in China as well as international project and cross-border supply experience.

This experience extends beyond buying products. It includes interpreting technical and commercial requirements, coordinating manufacturers and project stakeholders, comparing supply options, planning documentation and quality controls, and managing export, freight and delivery interfaces.

Important distinction: this experience was gained through the team members' previous corporate roles. It is presented as team experience, not as a 20-year HONVAX company history or as projects contracted by HONVAX.

TEAM CAPABILITY

HOW WE WORK TOGETHER

Experienced judgement with clearly defined responsibilities

HONVAX starts with the actual purchasing requirement and develops the sourcing route around the product, technical information, commercial priorities and delivery conditions.

Before an order proceeds, the quotation and contract identify the legal seller, payment route, supply scope, exclusions, delivery responsibilities and applicable terms. Proposed capability is kept separate from confirmed supply arrangements.
