Before Quotation

Used equipment requires machine-specific due diligence.

Model brochures do not prove the identity or condition of an individual machine. For a formal offer, the exact unit, nameplate, available photographs or video, operating information, location, inspection scope and delivery basis should be confirmed.

Define the requirement

Preferred brand, model or operating class, application, budget range and delivery destination.

Confirm the unit

Match available equipment to its identification, photos, available video and seller-provided information.

Agree the review scope

Inspection, condition reporting and additional checks can be agreed before purchase where practical.
