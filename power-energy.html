Sourcing Decision

The right supply route depends on more than factory price.

Stronger candidates

  • Planned packages with clear specifications and drawings
  • Products supported by the required technical evidence
  • Adequate time for technical approval, production and shipping
  • Order value sufficient to justify freight, testing and documentation

Requires early caution

  • Utility-approved items where the acceptance route is unclear
  • Highly project-specific electrical equipment with incomplete test evidence
  • Urgent requirements with insufficient approval or delivery time
  • Equipment dependent on rapid local commissioning or after-sales service
