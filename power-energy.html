WHAT THE RFQ SHOULD DEFINE

Information needed before a reliable sourcing decision

Missing information becomes a quotation risk. HONVAX identifies clarification points before supplier comparison.

01

Ratings and duty

Voltage, current, fault level, load, duty cycle and required performance.

02

Standards

Applicable IEC, SANS, utility, employer or project-specific requirements.

03

Environment

Altitude, temperature, corrosion, pollution, wind and installation conditions.

04

Evidence

Required type tests, routine tests, certificates, drawings and data schedules.

05

Project constraints

Approved brands, local-content requirements, delivery dates and site support.

SOURCING DECISION

A good import opportunity must work technically and commercially

Stronger candidates

  • Clear specifications and measurable acceptance criteria
  • Planned procurement with adequate manufacturing and shipping time
  • Quantities or package value that justify supplier development and freight
  • Products supported by established Chinese manufacturing capability

Requires early caution

  • Utility-restricted or approved-brand-only products
  • Urgent replacement equipment needed for an outage
  • Small orders with high certification or logistics cost
  • Equipment dependent on rapid local service or proprietary spares
