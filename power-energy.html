What the RFQ Should Define

Technical information that changes the decision.

Ratings & duty

Voltage, current, fault level, mechanical loading and operating duty.

Specifications & acceptance

Applicable IEC (International Electrotechnical Commission), SANS (South African National Standards), utility or project requirements; approved brands and equivalent-product rules.

Tests & documents

Type-test evidence, routine tests, drawings, certificates and the required acceptance documentation.

Environment & interfaces

Altitude, temperature, installation environment, cable or conductor compatibility and connection dimensions.

Local content & support

Project local-content requirements, commissioning, spares, warranty and after-sales responsibilities.

Sourcing Decision

Confirm acceptance before committing to supply.

Stronger Candidates

  • Planned packages with clear ratings and specifications
  • Products supported by the required test evidence
  • Adequate time for approvals, production and shipping

Requires Early Caution

  • Utility-approved equipment with an unclear acceptance route
  • Unverified equivalents or incomplete technical evidence
  • Urgent duties dependent on rapid local commissioning or service
