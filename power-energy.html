When China sourcing makes sense

Commodity or project packages with clear specifications, adequate lead time and sufficient value are the strongest candidates. Utility-approved items, highly project-specific equipment and products requiring local service need earlier compliance review.

Bid decision principle

Proceed only when specification compliance, landed cost, lead time, payment exposure and after-sales responsibility are commercially acceptable together.
