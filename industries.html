INITIAL ASSESSMENT

Industry fit does not automatically mean import fit

A product may fall within a core sector and still be better sourced locally. The actual RFQ determines whether a China supply route is practical.

01

Technical fit

Specifications, drawings, standards, approvals and operating conditions.

02

Order economics

Quantity, freight, duties, handling, payment exposure and total landed cost.

03

Local alternatives

Availability, price, delivery speed, local-content rules and service support.

04

Execution risk

Lead time, quality control, documentation, spares and after-sales responsibility.
