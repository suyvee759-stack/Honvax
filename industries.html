INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC REVIEW

What changes from one sector to another

The sourcing method is consistent, but the technical evidence, approval route, logistics and operating risks are not the same in every industry.

01

Power & Energy

Ratings, fault levels, environmental conditions, project or utility specifications, type-test evidence, approved brands and local-content requirements may determine whether an offer is acceptable.

Stronger sourcing case

Planned packages with clear specifications, adequate approval time and products supported by the required technical evidence.

View industry detail →
02

Water & Municipal

Pressure class, diameter, face-to-face dimensions, body and seal materials, coatings, potable-water requirements, actuation and testing must be confirmed against the project documents.

Stronger sourcing case

Standardised valve, pipe, fitting and metering packages where quantities justify freight and local service dependence is limited.

View industry detail →
03

Mining & Industrial

Drawings, tolerances, material composition, hardness, heat treatment, operating conditions, traceability and sample approval are often more important than a general product description.

Stronger sourcing case

Repeat wear parts and planned maintenance packages with reliable drawings, samples or known references and a workable verification method.

View industry detail →
04

Infrastructure & Construction

Material grade, dimensional standards, welding, coating, fabrication tolerances, mill certificates, packaging and container loading affect both compliance and delivered cost.

Stronger sourcing case

Consolidated, specification-led material packages where volume, fabrication value or project coordination can support the import case.

View industry detail →
