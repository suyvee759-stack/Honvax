Why It Matters

A supplier listing is not the same as a supply solution.

Factories that appear to make the same product can differ greatly in technical capability, export experience, quality systems and willingness to support project documentation. Supplier development is built around the actual RFQ.

01

Supplier identity

Manufacturer or trader status, legal entity, location and business continuity.

02

Technical capability

Relevant product range, equipment, processes, engineering support and test capability.

03

Project evidence

Similar supply history, export experience, certificates and project references where available.

04

Commercial fit

MOQ, lead time, payment terms, warranty, packing and willingness to support the RFQ.
