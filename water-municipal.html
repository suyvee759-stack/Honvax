When China sourcing makes sense

Standardized valve, pipe, fitting and metering packages can be good candidates when quantities justify freight. Emergency repairs, small quantities and equipment dependent on rapid local service are often better sourced locally.

Bid decision principle

Proceed only when specification compliance, landed cost, lead time, payment exposure and after-sales responsibility are commercially acceptable together.
