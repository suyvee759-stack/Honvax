WHAT THE RFQ SHOULD DEFINE

Information needed before a reliable sourcing decision

Early clarification reduces dimensional, material, approval and operational risk.

01

Service conditions

Water type, pressure, temperature, flow, operating frequency and installation environment.

02

Materials

Body, pipe, lining, coating, seal and corrosion-protection requirements.

03

Dimensions

Diameter, pressure class, face-to-face, flange drilling and connection interfaces.

04

Standards and approvals

Applicable SANS, ISO, EN, potable-water or client-specific requirements.

05

Testing and support

Inspection, pressure testing, documentation, spares, warranty and commissioning needs.

SOURCING DECISION

The right supply route depends on more than factory price

Stronger candidates

  • Planned valve, pipe, fitting or metering packages
  • Repeatable specifications and clear acceptance criteria
  • Quantities that justify freight, inspection and documentation
  • Projects with adequate production and shipping time

Requires early caution

  • Emergency repair items required immediately
  • Small mixed quantities with low total value
  • Products requiring unconfirmed potable-water approval
  • Equipment dependent on rapid local commissioning or service
