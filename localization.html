B-BBEE Localization & SKD Optimization

Engineering supply chains to meet South African local content thresholds and public sector tender requirements.

SKD Assembly Architecture

We pioneer Semi-Knocked Down (SKD) and Component-Level supply models. By manufacturing core technical elements (like PCBA modules, valve cores, and sensor housings) in China and executing final assembly, calibration, and packaging in South Africa, we maximize your local value score.

DTI Local Content Thresholds

South African public infrastructure tenders demand strict adherence to Department of Trade, Industry and Competition (DTI) designated sectors. Our packages are structured to clearly delineate local services, secondary finishing, and imported core value.

Downstream Enterprise Integration

We actively partner with local South African service workshops, galvanizing plants, and black-empowered logistics providers. This integration transfers tangible economic value downstream, strengthening your overall B-BBEE procurement metrics.
