The procurement process

Exact steps vary by project, but the decision points remain consistent.

1

Submit the requirement

Share the RFQ, BOQ, specifications, drawings, quantities, delivery location and required date.

2

Initial review

We identify the sourcing fit, material gaps, key risks and the most sensible next step.

3

Agree the commercial route

Where the opportunity is viable, we define whether HONVAX will quote as supplier or provide agreed procurement support.

4

Source and clarify

Suitable manufacturers are approached and technical or commercial differences are resolved.

5

Compare and quote

A structured offer is prepared around compliance, scope, price, lead time, delivery basis and exclusions.

6

Order and quality planning

After an order, specifications, approvals, inspections, documents and production milestones are controlled.

7

Export and delivery

Packaging, freight, clearance responsibilities and final delivery are coordinated according to the agreed terms.

8

Close-out and repeat

Delivery records, issues and future requirements are captured to improve the next purchasing cycle.

What improves quotation quality

More complete inputs produce faster and more reliable sourcing results.

Documents

Technical information

BOQ, specifications, drawings, datasheets, standards, approved brands and equivalent rules.

Commercial

Purchasing information

Quantities, delivery location, required date, Incoterm preference and payment requirements.

Project

Decision context

Tender status, bid closing date, approval process, local-content requirements and acceptable alternatives.
