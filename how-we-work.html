THE OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Each stage must produce enough evidence for the next decision

The process does not assume that every RFQ should proceed to supplier sourcing or that every China quotation should become an order.

At each stage, HONVAX checks whether the requirement remains technically feasible, commercially sensible and executable within the available time and responsibilities.

FOUR CONTROLLED STAGES

The procurement route

Exact activities depend on the product, project stage, technical risk and commercial arrangement.

01

ReviewDefine what is actually required

KEY ACTIONSReview the RFQ, BOQ, specifications, drawings, quantities, destination and required date. Identify gaps and clarification points.

STAGE OUTPUTA clearer requirement and an initial decision on whether China sourcing should be developed.

→02

SourceDevelop the appropriate China supply route

KEY ACTIONSIdentify and screen manufacturers for relevant capability, experience, evidence, lead time and commercial fit.

STAGE OUTPUTA qualified supplier route and coordinated RFQ engagement based on the clarified requirement.

→03

CompareMake differences and risks visible

KEY ACTIONSNormalize supplier responses and compare compliance, deviations, scope, evidence, terms, timing and delivered cost.

STAGE OUTPUTA structured basis for supplier selection, quotation strategy or a decision not to proceed.

→04

ExecuteControl the approved route through delivery

KEY ACTIONSCoordinate ordering, approvals, production milestones, inspection, documents, packaging, export and delivery.

STAGE OUTPUTAn executed supply package with agreed quality, logistics and close-out records.

DECISION GATES

Three questions remain active throughout the process

01

Technical fit

Does the proposed product meet the actual specification, application and evidence requirements?

02

Commercial sense

Does the complete delivered cost, lead time, payment exposure and order value justify the route?

03

Execution readiness

Are quality controls, responsibilities, documents, logistics and after-sales obligations sufficiently clear?

WHAT IMPROVES THE RESULT

Better inputs produce faster and more reliable sourcing

The process can begin with incomplete information, but missing inputs must be identified and resolved before they create quotation or order risk.

01

Scope

RFQ, BOQ, quantities and clear supply boundaries.

02

Technical

Specifications, drawings, data sheets and standards.

03

Acceptance

Approved brands, alternatives, tests and inspection needs.

04

Commercial

Required date, destination, delivery basis and payment requirements.

05

Project context

Tender stage, approval route, local content and decision deadline.

COMMERCIAL ROUTE

The working arrangement is agreed before detailed execution

Initial review

  • Determine whether China sourcing is worth developing
  • Identify major risks and missing information
  • Recommend the most sensible next step
  • Advise local sourcing when it is clearly more suitable

Formal engagement

  • HONVAX product quotation and supply
  • Agreed procurement or sourcing support
  • Quality, inspection and export coordination
  • Logistics and delivery support under agreed responsibilities
