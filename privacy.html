Privacy Policy

How HONVAX handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 3 September 2026.

Information we collect

When you contact HONVAX or submit an RFQ, we may receive your name, company, business contact details, project information and documents you choose to provide.

How we use the information

We use submitted information to review, source, quote and communicate about the relevant procurement requirement, and for reasonable business administration connected with that enquiry.

Commercial documents

RFQs, BOQs, drawings, specifications and related commercial information are treated as commercially sensitive and used for evaluating and executing the relevant enquiry. If a project requires a separate confidentiality agreement, this should be agreed in writing.

Service providers

Website hosting, email, form-processing or other service providers may process information on our behalf to operate the website and respond to enquiries. The replacement package supplied with this site is configured to use FormSubmit as the default static-form processor unless HONVAX replaces that endpoint with another approved form service or first-party backend.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, business records, legal obligations or legitimate commercial follow-up.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information held in connection with an enquiry, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.
