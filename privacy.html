Information handling

Last updated: 3 September 2026.

Information we receive

We receive information you choose to submit, such as your name, company, contact details, project information and uploaded documents.

Why we use it

We use this information to assess enquiries, prepare responses, communicate about potential supply or procurement work, maintain business records and protect the website from misuse.

Sharing and storage

Information may be handled by service providers used for website forms, email, file storage or business administration. Relevant project information may be shared with prospective suppliers where necessary for an agreed sourcing enquiry. Tell us about disclosure restrictions or request a non-disclosure agreement before sending sensitive material. Do not submit confidential information unless you are authorised to do so.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct or request deletion of information held by HONVAX, subject to legitimate record-keeping needs. Contact info@honvax.com.

Security and changes

No online transmission is completely risk-free. HONVAX may update this notice when its website or business processes change.
