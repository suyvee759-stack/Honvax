Privacy

Website privacy notice.

This notice explains how information submitted through the HONVAX website is used for business enquiries.

Information submitted

We may receive contact details, company information, RFQs, BOQs, specifications, drawings and other information that you choose to provide in connection with a business enquiry.

Purpose

Information is used to review and respond to enquiries, assess sourcing requirements, prepare quotations and support agreed procurement activities.

Commercial information

Procurement documents are treated as commercially sensitive and are shared only as reasonably necessary for the relevant sourcing, quotation or supply activity.

Third-party form service

The website RFQ form uses Formspree to transmit submissions. Users may alternatively send documents directly to info@honvax.com.

Contact

Questions about website privacy may be sent to info@honvax.com.
