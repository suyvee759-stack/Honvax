Regulatory Compliance & Risk Mitigation

Zero-tolerance standards alignment for South African municipal, TCTA, and Eskom-connected infrastructure projects.

SABS / SANS Standards

Every engineering package we supply maps strictly to South African National Standards (e.g., SANS 1123, SANS 509, SANS 1700). We eliminate port clearance failures by pre-verifying compliance dossiers before dispatch from Chinese manufacturing hubs.

Mill Test Traceability

For structural steel, pipelines, and high-pressure components, we mandate complete Heat Number tracking and Mill Test Certificates (MTC) in alignment with EN 10204 3.1 standards, ensuring full accountability on site.

Tariff & Anti-Dumping Defense

Navigating SARS and ITAC regulations requires precision. We deploy advanced HS code isolation engineering to classify components cleanly under finished machinery parts, protecting your budget from arbitrary raw steel anti-dumping duties.
