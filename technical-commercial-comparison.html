WHY IT MATTERS

The lowest quoted price may represent a different scope

Supplier quotations often exclude different items, use different assumptions or respond only partially to the specification.

A meaningful comparison makes those differences visible. HONVAX separates confirmed compliance from deviations and unanswered points, while also comparing lead time, payment exposure, warranty and the costs required to deliver the product.

IMPORTANT LIMIT

Unconfirmed information remains visible

Confirmed comparison

  • Supplier response supported by documents or clarification
  • Like-for-like quantities and commercial basis
  • Known delivery and landed-cost assumptions
  • Recorded deviations and exclusions

Items requiring decision

  • Missing technical evidence
  • Alternative materials or standards
  • Client acceptance of deviations
  • Unresolved logistics, warranty or service exposure
